      Keyboard & help

      Quick help

      • Pen: click = corner, drag = curve, click first point to close · right-click = type exact length & angle
      • Select: click to select, drag the selection to move it · double-click point = corner↔smooth · double-click edge = add point
      • Handles: a selected point shows its own handles plus the hollow ones of its neighbours — click one (it rings) and Del takes just that handle, or double-click it / drag it onto its point
      • Click an edge, then type a length in the sidebar to resize it
      • Right-click an edge → divide it at a distance, percentage, or into equal parts
      • Weld: click an edge on each of two pieces to fuse them into one (the seam disappears)
      • Fold: select a straight edge → "Make fold line" — draft half, the mirror updates live
      • Offset → Guide line: click edges (or inside a piece) to create a stitching guide
      • Stitch: Create runs on edges/guides/cutouts; Edit selects holes explicitly. Matched mode pairs two sides.
      • Knife: click two points (or an open path) to cut a piece in two
      • Boolean: overlap two pieces → union / subtract / intersect
      • Shape: drag rectangles/ellipses · dragging pieces magnets onto other points
      • Pieces list: double-click a name — or hit the ✎ — to rename it in place (Enter saves, Esc cancels)
      • Export: tick Engrave piece names to burn each name inside its piece (single-stroke, MARK layer, DXF + SVG)
      • Snapping: points, the middle of every edge (◇ marker, measured along the curve), anywhere on another outline, then grid
      • Del removes point · scroll/two-finger pans · pinch or Ctrl+scroll zooms · Space+drag pans
      • Solid outlines, holes and stitch slots = CUT. Dashed guide lines and grainlines = MARK.
